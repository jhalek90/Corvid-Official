# Corvid trademark policy

This is a short, plain policy for the Corvid name and artwork. It is not legal advice.

## What is covered

"Corvid"™, the Corvid logo and the crow artwork are trademarks claimed by the licensor named in the
[LICENSE](LICENSE) file. They are not registered trademarks.

The code license ([LICENSE](LICENSE), the Functional Source License 1.1 with the Apache 2.0 future license) covers
the code. It does not give you any rights to these trademarks, apart from displaying the license details and
naming us as the origin of the software, as the license itself says.

## What you may do

- Say that your project works with Corvid, extends Corvid, or is built on Corvid, in plain words (for example
  "an extension for Corvid" or "a fork of Corvid").
- Refer to Corvid by name when you write about it, review it or teach it.

## What you may not do

- Distribute a fork or a modified copy of Corvid under the name "Corvid", or under a name or logo so close to it
  that people could mistake it for the official one. A fork or a modified distribution needs its own name.
- Use the Corvid logo or the crow artwork in a way that suggests your project is the official Corvid, or that it
  is made, approved or endorsed by the Corvid project.

## Keep the notices

When you share Corvid or a modified copy, keep the [LICENSE](LICENSE) file and the copyright and licence
notices, as the license requires.

## Claude and Anthropic

"Claude" is a trademark of Anthropic. Corvid is an independent project. It is not made by, endorsed by or
affiliated with Anthropic.

## Questions

If you are not sure whether a use is fine, ask the maintainer before you publish it: open an issue on
[Corvid's issues page](https://github.com/jhalek90/Corvid-Official/issues).
